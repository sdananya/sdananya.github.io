Hi! I'm Ananya Sutradhar. I enjoy building things, breaking things, and learning from both. 

I'm currently a [MATS](https://www.matsprogram.org/research) 10.1 Fellow, working with [Prof. Shi Feng](https://shifeng.me/), working on AI safety and alignment. Previously I was an [Anthropic AI safety Fellow](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2024/anthropic-fellows-program/). I am also a [SPAR Fellow](https://sparai.org/), working with [Prof. Lionel Levine](https://lionellevine.github.io/) on Value Alignment and [Character Training](https://openreview.net/forum?id=oh9CqCyxSc).

Before this, I was a Research Fellow at [Microsoft Research India](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/) working with [Ravishankar Krishnaswamy](https://rakri.github.io/), [Gopal Srinivasa](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/gopalsr/) and [Navin Goyal](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=w8WJCnkAAAAJ&hl=en). I worked on efficient [multi-filter nearest neighbor retrieval](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.04073) and [evaluating Deep Research systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12171).

I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Technology from [IIEST Shibpur](https://www.iiests.ac.in/) in 2025. I also interned as a Software Development Engineer at Amazon during the summer of 2024.
